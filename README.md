# Business Automation Resources

Practical resources and guidance for businesses looking to reduce repetitive work, improve processes, and connect the tools they use every day.

## About This Repository

Business automation is not simply about replacing manual tasks with software. Effective automation starts with understanding how a process currently works, identifying unnecessary steps, and deciding where automation can provide a measurable improvement.

This repository collects practical ideas and resources for evaluating business processes and planning automation projects.

## Where Automation Can Help

### Lead Management

Instead of manually assigning every new lead, businesses can use defined rules to route leads based on factors such as location, product interest, source, or sales team.

### Follow-Up Tasks

Automated task creation can help sales and customer service teams maintain consistent follow-up schedules.

### Data Synchronization

Integrations can reduce duplicate data entry by synchronizing information between systems such as CRM, accounting, inventory, or other business applications.

### Notifications

Automated notifications can alert team members when important events occur, such as a new enquiry, overdue task, changed deal stage, or completed process.

### Reporting

Automated reports and dashboards can reduce the time spent collecting information manually and provide teams with more consistent access to business data.

## Before Automating a Process

A simple automation assessment should answer:

1. How does the process currently work?
2. How frequently is the process performed?
3. How much manual effort does it require?
4. Where do errors or delays occur?
5. What information is required?
6. Which systems are involved?
7. What should trigger the automated process?
8. What should happen after the trigger?
9. How will the result be monitored?

## Simple Automation Planning Framework

**Identify → Document → Simplify → Automate → Test → Monitor**

### 1. Identify

Choose a process that creates unnecessary manual work or delays.

### 2. Document

Record the current steps, people involved, systems used, and expected outcomes.

### 3. Simplify

Remove unnecessary steps before automating the process.

### 4. Automate

Choose appropriate workflows, integrations, scripts, or business applications.

### 5. Test

Test normal scenarios as well as exceptions and incomplete data.

### 6. Monitor

Review the automation regularly and make improvements as business requirements change.
